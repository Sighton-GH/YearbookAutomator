# F1.4 prep: outline and shadow

Partial, not integrated. TextStyle gains stroke_width 0-20, stroke_color #ffffff and shadow None or TextShadow(offset_x, offset_y, blur, color, opacity). `composite_text(image, draw_callback, shadow)` draws on full-size transparent RGBA, shifts alpha without wraparound, applies Gaussian blur and opacity and composites beneath foreground. RGB/RGBA modes are retained.

Integrator converts validated name_shadow/quote_shadow dictionaries using TextShadow(**dict), passes stroke fields, and routes warnings. Effects are output pixels and may extend beyond guide bounds; image edges clip them. Fitting measures glyph ink without stroke/shadow padding at this stage, so effects can overflow tight boxes. Unit tests check outline extent, shadow colour/opacity, and nonwrapping offset. Actual UI/strip/TIFF integration remains unverified.
